## Pack outline

1. Volumes by process/version (started, decided, in-flight aging).
2. Breaches: SLA, DQ (D38), statutory — with trend + owners.
3. Overrides: human-overturned auto steps, top reasons, repeat actors (SoD watch, B19).
4. Promotions/demotions: L3↔L4 moves with evidence-pack links (I83).
5. Incidents: Sev1/2 summaries, post-mortem status, action-item burn-down.
6. Model & rules: eval deltas, rule releases, red-team results (G67/G70/I84).
7. Financial: cost/instance, waiver totals, leakage exceptions (G69/E45).
